# Milestone 4 — Analytics and Forecasts

Extends the [bank/portfolio policies](MILESTONE_2_ARCHITECTURE.md) and [income/protection ledger](MILESTONE_3_ARCHITECTURE.md). No dependency, permission, provider, worker, upload or encryption/key change was introduced. Three modules remain: pure Kotlin finance, encrypted Room persistence, and native Compose. Forecasts and calculators run on Dispatchers.Default; Room writes remain transactional.

## Decimal mathematics and limits

FinancialMath/DecimalMath use BigDecimal throughout, including logarithm, exponential, square root and fractional powers. Transcendental approximations use **160 significant digits** with bounded iteration, sufficient guard precision for the supported 24-digit inputs and 100-year/1000%-annual growth envelope. Square root uses decimal Newton iteration because Android's BigDecimal.sqrt requires [API 33](https://developer.android.com/reference/java/math/BigDecimal#sqrt(java.math.MathContext)); minimum API 29 is retained. Float conversion is restricted to normalized chart coordinates, never accounting or persistence.

Effective monthly return is `(1 + annual)^(1/12) − 1`. Real annual return is `(1 + nominal)/(1 + inflation) − 1`; the inverse multiplies the growth factors. Annual growth assumptions must exceed -100% and be at most 1000%. Tool horizons are 1–1200 months; forecast horizons are at most 360 months. Inputs reject exponent/grouping syntax and unsupported currency precision. Outputs round HALF_EVEN at currency precision; percentage display uses three decimal places. Projection/annuity internals keep guard precision without rounding every hypothetical payment to paise. Loan interest and scheduled payments use explicit currency rounding. Calculators are estimates before tax, spreads, unentered fees and product-specific rules.

SIP payments occur at the selected beginning/end of month. Annual step-ups first affect month 13, then 25, etc. Goal contributions use end-of-month annuities and clamp required saving at zero if already funded. Inflation tools distinguish future cost from the present purchasing power of a future nominal amount. FD/RD tools reuse Contracts: nominal periodic compounding with simple ACT/365 stubs; RD installments start on the initial date at original-anchor plus whole months. EMI/amortization reuse the fixed nominal annual-rate/12 reducing balance, with final rounding residual cleared. Prepayment occurs after the selected installment, retains EMI, shortens term and subtracts the explicitly entered fee from interest savings; no statutory fee exemption is assumed.

Retirement corpus is a finite-horizon present value of monthly inflation-linked spending, beginning after retirement. Pre-retirement expense inflation and retirement withdrawal timing are shown. Withdrawal planning grows the corpus first, then withdraws at month end; the first partially funded month is exposed and only available corpus is counted as withdrawable. There is no lifetime adequacy or safe-withdrawal-rate promise. Currency conversion uses entered dated INR-per-unit references, not a market feed or execution price.

## Investment performance

Instrument analysis is in native currency and is scoped to unit-tracked investments. Contributed capital comprises known opening basis, fee-inclusive purchases and external reinvestment fees; reinvested gross distributions are internal rather than new external capital. Simple gain is `(current value + external sale/distribution proceeds − contributed capital) / contributed capital`, explicitly not annualised or time-weighted. FIFO realized/unrealized book gains retain the existing tax limitations. Unknown basis or adjustments leave simple return unavailable.

XIRR uses dated investor cash flows, including the terminal market value. Opening positions and adjustments mark earlier history incomplete, even if acquisition basis is known. They never become fabricated purchases for XIRR. Reinvestment has only its actual external fee cash flow. CAGR is shown only for a known single investment and terminal receipt/value without intervening flows; ACT/365 dates are exposed. A fully liquidated investment ends with its actual final receipt, not an invented terminal holding.

The unit-investment portfolio XIRR consolidates **each cash flow at its own eligible dated FX**, including terminal value at the valuation date. Missing FX/valuation/history returns unavailable. Carried FX older than seven days is flagged. Cash, property, deposits, retirement observations and loans are outside this performance scope. Net-worth movement uses actual dated wealth snapshots, including carried values/estimates/incompleteness, and is not labelled investment return.

XIRR follows [the documented ACT/365 XNPV-zero convention](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/excel/functions/xirr-function). Same-day flows are aggregated; zeros are removed. A single chronological sign change establishes uniqueness. Multiple sign changes conservatively return unavailable because uniqueness is not established, even when a numerical solver might find a candidate root. Bisection solves in `ln(1+r)` over -20 through 20, with a bounded 220 iterations, normalized NPV tolerance 1e-26 and interval tolerance 1e-25. No bracket, absent signs/dates, excessive history or non-convergence yields an explicit status. This deliberately avoids choosing a convenient root from an ambiguous series. The published Microsoft example is a reference fixture, not app demo data.

## Monthly forecast

Starting balances/holdings/liabilities come from WealthValuation at the saved start date. Missing starting values or FX make the projection unavailable; input names, native values, dates and quality are exposed. With no financial records, the planning model starts from zero; it does not seed a dashboard balance. Saved scenarios are assumptions, not frozen valuation snapshots: later corrections to financial records intentionally affect a recalculation at that date.

Each step uses the original start date plus whole months. Order: grow existing cash/assets, receive net salary, pay modeled living costs/premiums/loans, add planned contributions, then apply explicit withdrawals and active goals due within that step. Salary and SIP step-ups use annual anniversaries from month 13. First-month expenses equal the entered budget; monthly expense inflation applies thereafter. Today's purchasing power deflates closing nominal wealth over elapsed months from the scenario start.

* **Contribution mode:** additions and loan payments are externally funded; salary, living costs and premium budgets are outside the model. Contributions add new wealth once. Loan principal declines from externally funded payments while projected interest remains a cost to wealth.
* **Cash-flow mode:** net salary is added once, outflows are paid once, and contribution amounts move native cash into investments without creating wealth. Unused cash remains in its own currency. Recorded monthly summaries and policy installments are not automatically reposted on top of the entered budget.

Bank cash stays in native-currency pools. Asset returns are native-currency assumptions; FX changes independently compound INR per native unit. Positive cash earns the entered cash return. Native obligations require native cash: there is no invented execution-level currency conversion. Negative cash, an overdrawn named asset or an invalid withdrawal source produces a funding gap and stops the projection. No future outcome beyond that gap is claimed, and no automatic loan or sale fills it.

Existing FD/RD values project contractual increments from the starting observed/estimated value. Future RD installments must be entered explicitly; FDs accept no recurring additions. Future interest is assumed retained until maturity; actual coupons, withholding and bank-specific penalties are not guessed. Maturity is recognized at the monthly step that crosses its recorded date. The modeled value either transfers to same-currency cash or is explicitly reinvested at the saved asset return. It is never counted in both. Loans use recorded fixed contract rates, monthly rounded interest and explicit planned total payments, capped at the amount owed. Payments below interest increase the projected liability, flagged as an estimate; floating resets/arrears rules are not inferred.

The generic new-investment bucket and per-asset contributions are separate. Missing asset overrides use the scenario's general investment return with zero new contribution/payment and a warning. Explicit withdrawals use INR cash or a named non-contract asset in native currency. Shared active goals are withdrawn once when due only if the scenario enables them; an active overdue goal requires review rather than being silently skipped. Inactive/completed goals never create contributions or withdrawals. No earmark or coverage amount is added to assets.

The comparison screen applies a common selectable 5/10/15/20/30-year horizon while exposing each scenario's independent start date and mode. Details use the saved horizon and expose monthly/annual steps, assumptions, starting inputs, funding limits and revision history. No scenario is ranked as a prediction or recommendation.

## Persistence and goals

Additive encrypted **schema 4→5** creates `forecast_scenarios`, `forecast_assets`, `forecast_fx`, `forecast_withdrawals`, and `financial_goals`. All 21 previous table definitions remain unchanged. UUID scenario/goal keys group immutable revisions, while each revision has a separate row UUID. Scenario child assumptions reference that revision and instruments with restrictive foreign keys. Unique key/revision constraints and serialized Room transactions protect revision allocation. Scenario plus all child rows/audit commits atomically; failures preserve the previous plan. Goal currency is immutable. Corrections require explanations and retain prior rows. No destructive fallback or financial record reset is used.

Goals distinguish a future nominal target from purchasing power at an explicit saved reference date. Reviewing an old goal does not rebase target inflation to a new “today.” Declared earmarks are planning inputs, not live progress inferred from assumed past returns; their reference date and review limitation are shown. Remaining monthly contributions use the declared amount unchanged at review, then project future growth. Due/current-month targets show a direct comparison rather than inventing a fractional payment schedule. Completion/deactivation preserves history and has no cash posting. Shared goal definitions are recalculated from their latest revisions; scenario inclusion is independently saved.

## UI, privacy and remaining boundary

Forecast and Tools are native, functional destinations. Home also opens analytics. Form state survives activity recreation, and calculator output is invalidated when inputs change, avoiding stale results alongside edited inputs. Financial readouts/notes/charts obey amount obscuring; edit inputs intentionally remain visible. Demo scenarios/goals are explicitly fictional, read-only and memory-only. No fictitious data enters the personal vault.

Encryption, Keystore wrapping, access gate, screenshot protection and backup exclusions remain unchanged. Portable password-protected backup is still unavailable. Public providers remain Milestone 5; release, device/accessibility/performance matrices, portable backup/restore and independent security review remain Milestone 6. This remains a development-signed/debuggable APK.
